About Security Consulting Group

Security Consulting Group is an elite security group specialized in identifying potential risks for high-profile clients. The company can provide security and patrol officers qualified for day-to-day protection offerings.

SCG provides professional security services to clients, employees, patrons, and community members who visit or live at the sites it guards. The group embraces its role in providing public safety while protecting clients’ assets.

SCG’s Distinction

SCG aims to develop trusted business partnerships by providing technology-enabled solutions. With experience in security systems and guard services, the company works to protect clients from immediate, present, and future dangers.

Ambassadors of Protection: The catalog describes guards as trained professionals with certifications in CPR/First Aid, Baton, Pepper Spray, Firearms, and Power to Arrest.

Total Peace of Mind: Officers are presented as prepared to address site-specific security requirements and respond to emergencies.

Security Services & Solutions

Catalog services and descriptions arranged for easy browsing. The photos below are extracted from the supplied 2026 catalog.

Security Consulting Services

Planning and installing an updated security system is not always easy. SCG assesses personal security needs and proposes solutions that are effective and practical.

The catalog describes thorough designs and step-by-step consulting for security systems, whether upgrading an existing system or designing a new one from scratch.

Camera Systems & Installation

Modern camera systems help monitor who enters and leaves a building, deter break-ins, and gather evidence if a crime occurs. SCG offers camera systems and installation for projects of different sizes.

  • Protect property and valuables
  • Deter criminal activity
  • Allow remote viewing and access
  • May lower insurance costs
  • Reduce worry and stress

Alarm Systems & Installation

An up-to-date alarm system is described as a first line of defense for family, property, and valuables. Modern systems can deter burglars and alert authorities when emergency action is needed.

SCG sells and installs custom alarm systems using professional installation crews, with designs based on the client's security and budget needs.

  • Protect property and valuables
  • Deter criminal activity
  • Remote access
  • Gas or fire alerts
  • Reduce worry and stress

Fire Watch & Safety Services

SCG describes certified safety services including fire prevention, first aid, evacuation assistance, and emergency planning. On-site Fire Watch officers also perform additional safety services.

  • Evacuation assistance
  • Workplace and traffic safety
  • School safety
  • Safety consulting and compliance audits
  • Firefighting training
  • Emergency planning and crisis management
  • Evacuation exercises

Marked Vehicle Patrol

SCG describes vehicle patrol as a cost-effective deterrent against vandalism and theft. The catalog states that 24-hour dispatch is available and gives an average vehicle-patrol response time of 30 minutes or less.

Patrol officers monitor premises, identify hazards and suspicious activity, and provide personalized attention to each property.

Virtual Security

Virtual security combines smart cameras, people-detecting analytics, and a remote video monitoring center to help identify emerging threats and reduce reliance on conventional on-site guards.

  • Technology-enabled monitoring
  • Multiple areas monitored simultaneously, 24/7
  • Response to criminal activity
  • Professionally trained monitoring personnel
  • Reduced cost compared with a full-time security detail

On-Site Uniformed Security Officer

Uniformed security officers trained in crisis management can provide an immediate sense of safety for properties of different sizes. SCG describes guard services for businesses and individuals throughout California.

Services are described for retail properties, religious institutions, hospitals, schools, shopping centers, apartments, childcare facilities, construction sites, healthcare centers, gated communities, hotels, and private residences.

Armed Security Services

The catalog describes armed and unarmed security officers as a visible deterrent and a response resource at client locations. Officers may respond to emergency calls, unusual situations, and site-specific patrol assignments.

Tasks include checking for unsafe conditions, hazards, unlocked doors, security violations, and unauthorized entry, as well as monitoring entrances, exits, people, vehicles, and parking areas.

Security Guard Tasks

Security personnel focus on site-specific goals and procedures, including:

  • Checking credentials for restricted areas
  • Checking for contraband on business premises
  • Monitoring suspicious activity that could lead to theft or burglary
  • Monitoring and securing business areas after office hours
  • Opening and closing the business each workday

Construction Site Security

Construction sites can face vandalism, graffiti, theft, production delays, and financial loss. SCG describes customized plans and professional guards to help prevent unauthorized visitors from entering a site.

Guards patrol the site, while supervisors visit each shift to check that officers are performing their duties to the client's expectations.

Business Park Security

Business park security helps protect tenants, buildings, assets, and the people who work at the property. The catalog highlights maintaining a consistent security presence during business hours and additional overnight measures.

Shopping Center Security

SCG describes tailored security tactics for shopping centers, malls, and retail stores to help minimize loitering, theft, vandalism, trespassing, and other unwanted activity.

The catalog emphasizes experienced, licensed officers and solutions designed around the requirements of retail establishments.

Healthcare Facilities Security

Healthcare facilities have patients, medical professionals, suppliers, visitors, and service providers moving through the site at all hours. SCG describes security support designed for these open, active environments.

Professionally trained guards help prevent and respond to unwanted disputes and situations between different parties.

Hotel / Motel Security

SCG describes hotel and motel security guards as focused on the safety of guests and customers while representing the property with professional and reliable service.

The catalog notes that solutions are customized to each client's site-specific safety and security purposes.

Apartments / HOA Security Services

Apartment and condominium properties can be targets for theft, trespassing, loitering, vandalism, and burglary. SCG describes security services intended to help protect residents, tenants, owners, and property.

Apartments / HOA — Response & Patrol

The catalog describes guards trained to respond to emergencies and coordinate with local authorities when needed. SCG lists alarm response, on-site security guards, and vehicle patrols as available solutions.

Educational & Institutional Security

SCG describes campus security with trained security experts, risk consulting, and technology solutions to help create a safe environment for students, faculty, and staff.

  • Incident management and safety patrols
  • Parking enforcement and student escorts
  • Visitor access control
  • Lock-out assistance
  • Program design and management support

Industrial Security

SCG describes tailored industrial security services based on facility needs, with teams trained to recognize safety hazards and respond appropriately.

Additional services listed in the catalog include extinguisher checks, visitor reception and badging, employee credentialing, visitor escorts, job safety analyses, emergency preparation, loss prevention, mail and package distribution, and lighting inspections.

Security Patrol & Site Support

SCG's catalog also describes site access control, additional personnel when needed, scheduled or random patrol dispatch, premises supervision, inspections, alarm response, and timely reporting.
